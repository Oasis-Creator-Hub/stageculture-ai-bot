Villa of the
Two·Fishes

Triclinium pavement, Carvanum on the Sile, second century

Found under a metre of river silt, the dining-room floor shows a pair of fish forever chasing one another round a small sun. The steward of the house, one Lucius Varro Tesserarius, signed nothing; the cable border is his signature.
