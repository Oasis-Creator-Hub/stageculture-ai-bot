How a Loam piece happens

Three weeks, two firings, one stubborn kiln called Marguerite.

  1. Crush

    Every cracked bowl and trimmed foot is dried, hammered and sieved into chips: clay red, sage, oat and bone.

  2. Fold

    Chips are wedged into a cream stoneware body by hand, then thrown or slab-built while the mix is still arguing.

  3. Reveal

    After the bisque we grind the surface back with wet stone until the chips open like cut fruit, then glaze clear.
