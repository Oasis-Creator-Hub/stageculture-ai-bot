A grotesk that refuses to hold still.

Tensile was drawn on rubber. Every stem is a spring under load, every bowl a held breath; the outlines you see at rest are only the place the letters agree to return to. Run a cursor or a thumb through the word above and it will lean away, lengthen, and snap back with whatever dignity it can find.

Three tensions ship in the family. Slack sulks. Taut is the text cut, polite until provoked. Overstrung should not be set near deadlines, glassware or nervous clients.
