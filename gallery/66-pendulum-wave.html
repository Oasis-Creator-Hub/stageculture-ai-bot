The Pendulum Wave

Each bob hangs a little shorter than its neighbour, and so swings a little quicker. Released together they unravel into travelling waves, split into braided strands, dissolve into apparent chaos — and then, exactly on schedule, fall back into line.

What you are seeing

Above, the row seen from a front corner; below, the same bobs seen from directly overhead, where the wave is plain. No bob ever touches or influences another. The pattern is entirely in your eye — a moiré made of time.

The arithmetic

In one full cycle the longest pendulum completes 51 swings, the next 52, then 53, and so on.Ln = g · ( T ⁄ 2π(51 + n) )²After T seconds every bob has made a whole number of swings and they meet again.
