Sign list of the reed-cutters · scroll the seventy-fifth

Write it in Tessat

The marsh people of Omu had twenty-six signs and no patience for vowels left unsaid. Give the scribe a word; a name is bound in its own ring, as names should be.

The twenty-six signs

Copied by the hand of Iru-Meket in the season of low water. Any resemblance to the writing of real river kingdoms is the fault of rivers.
