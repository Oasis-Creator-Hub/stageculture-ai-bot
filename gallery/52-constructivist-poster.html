Gantry No. 4 Workers' Theatre · Programme

Five plays built by hand.

Written on night shifts, rehearsed between tides, hammered together from crate-wood and borrowed lamps. No stars, no velvet, no interval bar. Touch a play and the poster will shout its name.

  1. 1
    14 Oct — 2 Nov

    The Night Shift Sings

    Forty stevedores, one stubborn foghorn, and a cargo nobody will admit to ordering. A chorus-play for the whole quay.

  2. 2
    9 Nov — 30 Nov

    Iron Bread

    The foundry canteen goes on strike over the soup. By the third act the soup has a committee of its own.

  3. 3
    7 Dec — 4 Jan

    A Strike in Three Bells

    Three bells, three walk-outs, one clock-tower that refuses to take sides. Performed in real time, with a real bell.

  4. 4
    18 Jan — 15 Feb

    The Crane-Driver's Wedding

    She will not come down until the banns are read at altitude. A comedy at forty metres, with ladders.

  5. 5
    1 Mar — 29 Mar

    Electric Spring

    The district is promised light by April. The apprentices decide not to wait. The finale uses every bulb we own.
