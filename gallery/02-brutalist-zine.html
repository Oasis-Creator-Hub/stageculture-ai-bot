Editorial

Stop sanding the world down.

Every surface you touch today was focus-grouped into a pebble. We would like our corners back.

This issue is about things that admit what they are made of. Stairwells that echo. Type that shouts because it has something to carry. A bus shelter that is, honestly, just four slabs and a decision. We photographed them badly on purpose, because good photographs lie.

If a page here is ugly, check whether it is also true. That is the only review we accept.
