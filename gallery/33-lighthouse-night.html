Keeper's Log

Station No. 33 · winter quarter · A. Tormund Hale, principal
3 Nov · 16:40wind NW 4 · glass 29.8

Lit at sunset and wound the clockwork. The lens turned as sweet as a kitchen clock. Supply boat stood off on account of swell; she signalled all well and went home with my letters still aboard.

9 Nov · 02:15wind W 7 · glass 29.1, falling

Sea breaking clean over the landing. Spray on the lantern panes forty metres up, which I had not thought possible. Wiped the glass on the half-hour. The light does not care what the weather thinks of it, and I am trying to learn the trick.

14 Nov · 21:05calm · fog, thick

Fog came in like a held breath. Started the horn at nine. Between blasts I could hear a ship's bell answering somewhere to the south, patient and polite, the two of us talking all night without one word in common.

15 Nov · 06:30calm · fog lifting

The bell passed east at first light. I never saw the vessel. Entered her as ship unknown, heard only. Slept four hours and dreamed the beam was a hand feeling along a shelf for something it had put down.

22 Nov · 17:10wind NE 3 · frost

Cleaned the prisms with spirit and a chamois. Counted them again: two hundred and eighty-eight, same as ever. A seal has taken the lower step as her own and regards me, correctly, as the lodger.

30 Nov · 23:50wind SW 5 · rain

Oil sufficient, wicks trimmed, mantle sound. Nothing to report, which on this rock is the best report there is. The light went round. Somewhere out there, somebody turned for home because of it.
