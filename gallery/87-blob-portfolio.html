AboutPart designer,
part jelly.

I’m Odalys. I trained as a ceramicist, which is why everything I draw looks like it was squeezed. For nine years I’ve helped small, stubborn companies look like nobody else on the shelf.

I work alone, answer my own emails, and take on six projects a year so each one gets my whole wobbly heart.
